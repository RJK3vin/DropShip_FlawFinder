Privacy Policy — DropShip FlawFinder

Last updated: August 2026

DropShip FlawFinder ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps e-commerce sellers analyze product reviews. This page explains what data the extension collects and how it's used.

What we collect

Email address (optional): if you choose to verify a paid subscription, the email you enter is sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker we operate) solely to check whether it's associated with an active subscription. We do not sell, share, or use this email for marketing.

We do not collect: browsing history, personal identifying information beyond the email described above, or any data from pages other than the product review pages you actively choose to scan.

Review content

When you click "Analyze Flaws," the extension reads publicly visible review text already displayed on the product page you're viewing. This content is processed entirely on your device using Chrome's built-in AI — it is not sent to any external server, and we do not store or retain it beyond your local browser storage.

Payment information

Subscription payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details — Stripe handles all payment data according to their own privacy policy and security standards.

Data storage

Your scan history, scan count, and subscription status are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage APIs. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us except as described above for subscription verification.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
